# 🎓 HITK Hub

> A student-focused academic platform designed to bring study resources, laboratory preparation, doubt solving, and peer-to-peer academic support into one organized platform.

**Project Status:** 🚧 Under Active Development

---

## 📌 About The Project

**HITK Hub** is a student-oriented academic platform being developed to make academic resources easier to find, organize, access, and share.

Students often depend on different WhatsApp groups, Google Drive links, personal messages, and scattered resources for notes, previous year questions, laboratory manuals, viva questions, and other study materials.

HITK Hub aims to bring these resources together in a single platform.

The platform is designed around the following academic structure:

Department  
↓  
Year  
↓  
Semester  
↓  
Subject  
↓  
Academic Resources

---

## 🎯 Project Goals

- Make academic resources easier to find
- Organize resources subject-wise
- Provide a dedicated laboratory preparation section
- Allow students to ask and solve academic doubts
- Provide previous year questions
- Provide suggestive and important questions
- Create a platform for sharing study materials
- Create a marketplace for student-created notes
- Enable peer-to-peer academic assistance
- Reduce dependency on scattered WhatsApp groups and file links
- Build a centralized academic community for students

---

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Student Dashboard

The dashboard will provide students with a personalized overview of their academic resources.

Students will be able to see:

- Department
- Year
- Semester
- Subjects
- Recently viewed resources
- Saved resources
- Recent doubts
- Purchased resources
- Academic activity

---

### 📚 Study Resources

Students will be able to access academic resources organized by department, year, semester, and subject.

Resources will include:

- 📖 Notes
- 📝 Previous Year Questions (PYQs)
- ⭐ Suggestive Questions
- ✅ Solutions
- 🔖 Saved Resources
- 🔍 Search and filtering

Students will first select:

Department  
↓  
Year  
↓  
Semester  
↓  
Subject

After selecting a subject, they will be able to access the available resources.

---

### 🧪 Lab Hub

HITK Hub will have a dedicated section for laboratory and practical preparation.

The Lab section will include:

- 📕 Lab Manuals
- 🧪 Lab Experiments
- 🎤 Viva Questions
- ⭐ Suggestive Questions
- 📝 Lab PYQs

Students will be able to navigate through:

Department  
↓  
Year  
↓  
Semester  
↓  
Lab Subject

Lab experiments may contain:

- Experiment number
- Experiment title
- Aim
- Requirements
- Theory
- Algorithm
- Procedure
- Program
- Output

---

### 🎤 Lab Viva Questions

Students will be able to prepare for practical examinations using subject-wise and experiment-wise viva questions.

Questions can be categorized according to:

- Experiment
- Difficulty
- Importance
- Topic

---

### ⭐ Suggestive Questions

The platform will provide important and suggestive questions for exam preparation.

These can be organized according to:

- Department
- Year
- Semester
- Subject
- Unit
- Topic
- Difficulty

---

### 📝 Previous Year Questions

The PYQ section will allow students to find previous year question papers based on their academic requirements.

Possible filters include:

- Examination year
- Semester
- Subject
- Examination type
- Department

---

### 💡 Doubt Section

HITK Hub will include a community-based academic doubt-solving section.

Students can:

- Ask questions
- Describe their problems
- Attach relevant images
- Answer other students' questions
- Mark accepted answers
- Search previous discussions
- Report inappropriate content

---

### 🛒 Notes Marketplace

HITK Hub will include a marketplace for student-created academic materials.

Students will be able to:

- Upload notes
- Add title and description
- Select subject
- Set their own price
- Offer free resources
- Browse resources
- Purchase resources
- View purchased resources
- Create seller profiles
- Receive ratings and reviews

Payment functionality will be implemented securely in a future version.

---

### 💼 Academic Help

HITK Hub will provide a peer-to-peer academic support system.

Students can request legitimate academic assistance such as:

- 🧑‍🏫 Concept explanations
- 💡 Problem-solving guidance
- 💻 Code debugging
- 📝 Feedback on drafts
- 📚 Research guidance
- 🎤 Viva preparation
- 👩‍💻 Peer tutoring

Students can create requests containing:

- Subject
- Topic
- Description
- Budget
- Deadline

Other students can make offers to provide academic assistance.

> HITK Hub is intended for academic guidance, tutoring, and learning assistance. It is not intended to facilitate submitting work completed by another person as one's own.

---

### 👤 Student Profiles

Each student will have a profile containing information such as:

- Name
- Email
- Department
- Year
- Semester
- Uploaded resources
- Purchased resources
- Doubts
- Answers
- Achievements
- Reviews

---

### 🏆 Leaderboard

A leaderboard system is planned to encourage positive academic participation.

Possible metrics include:

- Helpful answers
- Accepted answers
- Resource contributions
- Community activity
- XP
- Achievements

---

### 🛡️ Admin & Moderation

A dedicated administration system is planned to maintain the quality and safety of the platform.

Admins and authorized moderators will be able to:

- Manage users
- Manage departments
- Manage years and semesters
- Manage subjects
- Manage labs
- Review resources
- Approve resources
- Reject resources
- Handle reports
- Moderate doubts
- Manage marketplace content
- Monitor platform activity
